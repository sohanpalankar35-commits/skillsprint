    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about SkillSprint verification, deadlines, and internal registrations.

    What is the difference between "Participate" and "Apply on official page"?

    "Participate" registers your intent and college profile directly inside SkillSprint for roster tracking, mentor matching, and team coordination. "Apply on official page" redirects you straight to the host's portal to complete any mandatory external formal applications.

    Why do I need to sign in with a username?

    Your username links your saved opportunities, registrations, USN, and semester details to your browser session. It ensures your roster data stays intact without having to type your college credentials repeatedly.

    Are these opportunities open to students from any college?

    Yes! Every opportunity lists its exact eligibility rules (e.g., Any Year, 1st Year Friendly, or Final Year). National hackathons and international fellowships accept students across all accredited institutions.

    How are deadlines kept accurate?

    Our student maintainers review listings continuously. Opportunities that expire are automatically marked past or cycled off the main board so students only view actionable deadlines.

    Can first-year students participate in hackathons and internships?

    Absolutely. You can use the "Open to 1st Years" filter in the search toolbar to isolate beginner-friendly hackathons, campus programs, and foundational workshops.

    How does the "Add deadline to calendar" feature work?

    When you click "Add deadline to calendar" inside any opportunity modal, SkillSprint generates a standard `.ics` iCalendar file that syncs directly with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook with an alert set for 24 hours prior.

    Can our college club feature a competition on SkillSprint?

    Yes. Simply drop a message through the contact form or email us at skillsprint@gmail.com with the competition rules, dates, poster, and registration link.

    Is there any subscription fee or registration charge?

    No. SkillSprint is 100% free and open-source for college students.

    Acknowledgment

    Why I built SkillSprint

    Every project begins with a small frustration, and SkillSprint began with one that I saw again and again around me: talented students missing opportunities not because they lacked ability, but because they never found out about them in time.

    Hackathons, internships, scholarships, fellowships, workshops and competitions are all announced somewhere, but rarely in one place. One deadline sits on a company careers page, another in a WhatsApp group, another on a poster in a corridor, and another in a link forwarded three times before it reaches you. By the time many students discover a chance that could have changed their year, or even their career, the last date has already passed. I wanted to put an end to that hunting and guessing. That is why I built SkillSprint: one board where every deadline is visible and ranked by what closes next.

    I created this website for the first-year student who doesn't yet know where to look, for the final-year student racing between applications and placements, and for every student who believes they can't compete because the information never reached them. Access to opportunity should not depend on who you know or which group chat you happen to be in. A student should be able to open one page, see what is closing this week, save what matters, add the date to a calendar, and act. That simple idea is the heart of this project.

    Building SkillSprint was also a journey of learning for me. It taught me to turn a real problem into a working product: to plan the structure, design the experience, write the code, test it, fix what broke, and improve it again and again. There were moments when things did not work, when a button would not respond or a layout looked wrong, and each of those moments taught me patience and the value of not giving up. Every fix made the platform better and made me a better builder.

    This is only the beginning. I hope to keep improving SkillSprint by adding more verified opportunities, better ways to track applications, and features that make student life a little easier and a lot more rewarding.
